About me

About me

I'm an Electrical Engineering student at Georgia Tech, concentrating in Circuit Technology and Bioengineering. I'm from Franklin, Massachusetts.

What drew me to EE is that you can work at every level, from code down to transistors. Software runs on a microcontroller, the microcontroller sits on a circuit board, and the chip itself is built from analog and digital circuits. So far I've mostly worked on boards and software, and I want to keep learning the layers underneath.

Most of my experience is in research. At Northeastern's Movement Neuroscience Lab, I wrote real-time software that controls lab equipment during experiments with human participants. Because those systems run on real people, I learned to think about safety and reliability from the start of a design.

Skills

  • Hardware: schematics, PCB layout, analog circuits
  • Software: C++, Python, real-time Linux, serial and TCP communication
  • Lab: oscilloscopes, multimeters, testing and debugging hardware

Outside of engineering

I tutor on Schoolhouse.world, a free online peer-tutoring platform, where I've led over 100 sessions. I also go rock climbing with friends and follow Formula 1.
