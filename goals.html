Career goals

Career goals

Long term, I want to be a hardware engineer designing electronics for products people rely on, and eventually lead the hardware design for one.

I'm most interested in work where analog circuits, digital logic, and software all come together in one system. That's where the hardest problems usually are, and it's the kind of work I've enjoyed most so far.

How I'll get there

  • Coursework: build a strong foundation in circuits, electronics, digital design, and signal processing through my Circuit Technology and Bioengineering threads.
  • Hands-on experience: keep doing research and gain industry experience so I learn how real products are designed, tested, and built.
  • Projects: keep building my own projects, like my EMG module, to practice design skills and learn new ones.
